Scientists Create the Woolly Mouse

Bio-engineering scientists have created the woolly mouse by combining woolly mammoth genes with mice genes to create a hybrid of mouse and mammoth. This was done in an effort to some day revive the woolly mammoth from extinction and bring them to the modern world.

Sea Turtles are laying eggs earlier in response to climate change

On March 7th, 2025, researchers monitoring the breeding habits of green and loggerhead turtles in Cyprus found that sea turtles are nesting earlier in response to rising temperatures. This adaptation is an effort to compensate for climate change.

Many animals are being hunted to extinction or affected by climate change. Female leatherback turtles are among the world’s most intrepid creatures, making journeys as far as 10,000 miles after nesting to find food in distant seas.
